Welcome. This is the first post on the blog — a short one, mostly to see how writing looks on the page.

## What you'll find here

Notes on the things I build, the data behind them, and the small experiments that don't fit anywhere else.

## How a post looks

Posts are written in plain text. You can use **bold**, *italics*, `inline code` and [links](https://github.com/BIOLIMON).

> A quote gets a calm accent line on its left.

Lists work too:

- one thing
- another thing
- a third thing

And so do numbered ones:

1. collect the data
2. clean it
3. make something out of it

Code blocks keep their formatting:

```python
for gene in genes:
    print(gene.name, gene.age)
```
